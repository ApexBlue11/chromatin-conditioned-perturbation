## WHAT THE TESTS CLAIM
- **The base is T-B off's:**
  - `test_base_inputs_are_tb_offs`: a v11 batch's base keys equal V10CData(chrom_off=True)'s, in every arm.
  - `test_pinned_base_files_are_tb_offs`: data_v10c and the modules are T-B's bytes. model_v10c differs from T-B's
    pin only by 100.8's `'dose_scale'` output key: undoing that line recovers T-B's sha1.
  - `test_base_init_is_v10c_under_the_same_seed_and_the_global_stream_is_unchanged`.
  - `test_forward_equals_tb_off_at_init_with_identical_base_weights[eval|train]`: torch.equal on delta, drug, basal and the
    dose scale, with dropout and stochastic depth on in train mode.
  - `test_trainer_recipe_is_train_v10cs`: with FG's readout frozen at zero and `--arm no_targets`, train_v11's predictions
    equal train_v10c --chrom_off's within 1e-6 after an epoch.
- **FG:**
  - it opens (cell and target information move delta, and basal is unchanged);
  - the factorised gate equals the explicit √a_i a_j edge weights;
  - its registered init values hold;
  - hop 1 matches 0.95 · Â t by hand.
- **The targets:**
  - known rows scatter their targets, and other rows take the head's probabilities on the vocabulary with sign 0;
  - the sign-combination rule;
  - probe targets never enter;
  - an unseen compound **and its same-molecule duplicate** are not "known";
  - the vocabulary comes from known compounds only;
  - the head loss is on known rows only;

- **The trainer and kernels:**

## ASKS
1. **The base.** Is the evidence enough for 102.1 C1's "R1 against T-B off measures FG alone", given these three
   points?
   - **The forked RNG:** the base's init and dropout stream are T-B off's at the same seed.
   - **The head's gradient:** in v11-full, −chromatin and −CCLE, the head's BCE trains `w_u` (part of the base). So R1
     measures FG + the head's auxiliary supervision. Only −targets removes the latter, which matches C4.
   - **Hardware:** T-B ran on the same docker image. GPU atomics make same-seed runs non-identical anyway.
2. **The detached head.** The predicted probabilities enter FG with a stop-gradient, so the head is trained only as a
   target predictor. Is that right? The alternative is to let the response loss shape it.
3. **"Known".** The design said "allowed". The code excludes probe compounds, unseen compounds and same-molecule duplicates
   of unseen compounds, and these get predicted targets. Is excluding the duplicates from the input (not only from the
   head's labels) right?
4. **The target universe:** only Homo sapiens genes in the graph (864 genes, 1,149 compounds; vocabulary 835 on the dev
   carve), not §102's "546". Compounds with only non-human targets count as unannotated. OK?
5. **The hop scales.** They are fixed per channel (the RMS of the nonzero landmark entries over the known compounds,
   ungated graph). Is this a sound, outcome-free normalisation? Should hop 0 rather be left at 1?
6. **Placement after h_basal.** FG never reaches the basal term, so a CCLE-only cell effect can act only through the drug
   path's tokens. Is that acceptable, or should the cell part (z) also enter the basal term?
7. **DataParallel.** FG's tables are a per-device cache shared through replicate()'s `__dict__` copy. Is that sound under
   DataParallel's threads? Only the timing kernel will exercise two GPUs.
8. **Before the timing kernel:** anything that must change before it runs. It runs at the quota reset (Sat 24 Oct
   00:00 UTC), before T-C's kernels.
