    C5 discharged: the module costs 1.009x per step, measured in a regime 34x off target
    
    Review 003's C5 pointed out the 5.6-hour figure scaled a rate measured with the
    module off, making it a floor of unknown tightness. Measured directly: 50
    optimiser steps per arm at full width on the local 3050 at batch 8. The module
    costs 1.009 times as much per step, 2.4858 seconds against 2.4636. Structurally
    that is expected, since the drug sequence is about 40 tokens against 978 gene
    tokens, so attention added on the drug side is a rounding error even at 30
    percent more parameters.
    
    But the measurement does not transfer cleanly and I am not quoting it as the
    projected cost. The local step is 2.464 seconds where Kaggle T4x2 at batch 48 is
    0.437, which is 5.6 times slower per step and 33.8 times slower per row. That is
    a memory-bound regime on 4 GB where a large fixed cost dominates, and a fixed
    cost compresses ratios, so the true ratio on Kaggle is plausibly larger.
    
    Defensible version: the cost is structurally small, measured at 1.009x in an
    unrepresentative regime, and the definitive figure is the first epoch of the real
    run. Budget 5.7 hours with a stated risk of more, and abort on the budget guard
    rather than on this estimate.
    
    Also documents a monitoring gotcha in watch_bus.ps1. Backgrounding it from a
    shell that appends anything after it returns the last command's status, so a
    TIMEOUT becomes indistinguishable from a NEW message in the harness
    notification. Propagate the exit code, or read line 1 of the output, which is
    always NEW, TIMEOUT or STOPPED.

    Diagonal-attention ablation operator, delegated and verified
    
    Implements review 003's C2. Mean-ablating _DrugBlock's output kills the SwiGLU
    branch along with the cross-atom mixing, making the ablated arm a
    smaller-capacity model and reintroducing the +30 percent parameter confound that
    staying inside one run was supposed to avoid. The operator that does not is
    diagonal attention: mask the attention matrix to the identity so each token
    attends only to itself, leaving parameters, normalisations, the SwiGLU branch and
    both residuals live, and removing only cross-token information flow.
    
    Written by a delegated worker under the 6b division -- I wrote the spec, the five
    behaviour tests and the exact verification contract; it wrote the body. Verified
    by me rather than by its report:
    
      test_drugsa_v9.py  11/11, including 'diagonal removes cross-atom flow:
        perturbing atom 3 moves atom 1 by exactly 0.00e+00', identical parameter
        count under diagonal, and 'not a no-op' at |d|max 1.1266 confirming the
        SwiGLU and residual are still live
      test_v9.py         55/55, unchanged
    
    The implementation is a _DrugAttention(QKNormAttention) subclass that delegates
    to super() when not diagonal, so the default path is bit-identical by
    construction rather than merely by test. It added two threading checks beyond the
    spec, for PerturbBlock and LincsV9.
    
    I also tested something the spec did not require: under diagonal plus key_mask a
    padded query row is entirely -inf and could softmax to NaN. Zero non-finite
    elements -- scaled_dot_product_attention handles it. A passing suite is not the
    same as an absent failure mode.
    
    The registry logs both attempts. The first returned exit code 0 having changed
    nothing and written no report, because it backgrounded its own tests, went idle,
    and terminated them on exit. Two lessons recorded: an agy exit code of 0 means
    the process ended rather than that the task succeeded, so always verify the
    artefact and never the exit code; and briefs must require every command to run in
    the foreground. That one-line fix turned a total failure into a clean success on
    the same model and the same task, which suggests most worker failures here are
    harness-shaped rather than capability-shaped.

    C4 gate: the atom-token effect is real and seed-stable, at half the size, on two of three splits
    
    RESULTS 57. Review 003's C4 demanded an interval and a cross-seed check before
    any GPU was bought. Cost: zero GPU-hours -- three fold0 checkpoints were already
    on disk, local-GPU inference only, and pearson_rows was copied verbatim from
    probe_v9.py so the intervals attach to that number. Bootstrap is over rows on the
    difference of medians, the statistic section 37 actually quotes, with the paired
    mean beside it. Chunking held at batch 48 because ablate_to_mean uses the chunk
    mean, so a different batch would be a different ablation.
    
    The gate passes on two splits and kills the third. On unseen_cell all three
    seeds span zero, so section 37's -0.00671 was noise. On unseen_compound and
    unseen_both every one of the three seeds excludes zero, so the effect is real.
    And the cross-seed check passes everywhere: seed-to-seed range is smaller than
    the within-seed interval on all three splits, which is the question probe_v9
    never asked.
    
    But the headline was inflated about 1.7 times. Section 37 reports -0.00671,
    -0.02549 and -0.02187 from one checkpoint at n=480; at n=1500 across three seeds
    the medians are -0.0029, -0.0146 and -0.0142. unseen_cell retracts to a null,
    unseen_compound halves, unseen_both shrinks.
    
    That leads to the first factual error in thirty challenges. C4 states 480 is 'the
    whole split, not a cap'. It cannot be: all three splits report exactly 480, drawn
    from strata of 47,002, 58,796 and 15,083 signatures, and this run finds at least
    1500 eligible rows in unseen_cell alone under identical filters. probe_v9.py was
    invoked with --n_eval 480 for a budget, and the cap is undocumented in the output
    JSON, which is why the reviewer read it as a population size. The error cuts for
    C4's conclusion rather than against it -- an arbitrarily small sample is exactly
    why the effect needed an interval. Review 003 stands at five of six upheld,
    running tally thirty-four of thirty-six. Fix owed: probe_v9.py must record n_eval
    and whether it bound.
    
    Also recorded: on unseen_cell seed 1 the two estimators differ in sign, d_median
    -0.00389 against paired_mean +0.00037, both near zero. That is what a null looks
    like under two estimators rather than a contradiction, but the paired mean is the
    tighter estimator throughout and would have been the better choice for section
    37. Per method rule 13 the estimator is fixed in advance as the difference of
    medians, because that is what the comparison must attach to.
    
    The spend is now justified, at a smaller predicted effect. Section 56.1 still
    stands -- the atom vectors are Uni-Mol atomic_reprs, already contextualised by a
    transformer with a 3D distance bias -- so the mechanism under test is a second
    in-loop recontextualisation rather than adding structure where there was none.
    The prediction is calibrated to -0.0146 rather than -0.025, on unseen_compound as
    the primary split, since unseen_cell has no effect left to move.
