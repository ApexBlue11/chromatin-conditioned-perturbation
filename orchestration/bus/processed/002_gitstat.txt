    RESULTS 52. The reviewer raised no new challenges but made an arithmetic point
    about action item 3, and it had one figure wrong in a way that strengthens its
    own conclusion.
    
    It read section 30's `0.005-0.045 on our cold splits'' as a standard deviation.
    Those are RANGES. The three-seed table is explicit: unseen cell range 0.0103 and
    sd 0.0052, unseen both range 0.0457 and sd 0.0232. Using the range span as an sd
    inflates the noise floor about twofold.
    
    Done with the directly relevant number -- our regime is unseen cell, whose seed
    sd this project measured in August at 0.0052 -- two independent single-seed runs
    differ with sd 0.0052 times root two, which is 0.0074. The observed chromatin
    effect is 0.0042, so it sits at 0.57 sigma of the run-to-run difference
    distribution. It is not unproven against an unmeasured floor; it is inside a
    floor we already measured, by a factor of two.
    
    One cold-cell arm costs 37,290 seconds. Three seeds per arm is six runs and 62
    GPU-hours, which is over two weeks of a 30-hour weekly quota, and it would only
    re-measure a spread section 21 already recorded. Powering an 0.0042 effect at
    sd_diff 0.0074 needs roughly 24 runs and 250 hours. Action item 3 is therefore
    dropped as a decision procedure, and if the this-split spread is ever wanted for
    the record it is a separate purchase that must be labelled as such.
    
    The reviewer's second point is simply correct: items 1 and 2 were listed
    separately but are one experiment, because the re-run produces exactly the saved
    predictions the placebo stratum needs. Revised to two runs and about 21
    GPU-hours, with save_pred, distinct filenames, args written into the JSON, and
    the comparison routed through head_to_head_mdmt.py so its row-alignment guards
    actually execute.
    
    Method rule 10, from the reviewer's generalisation: compute the direction of a
    selection bias, never assume it. Section 44 asserted that per-arm-best lambda
    favoured the chromatin arm and it had deflated it instead. Rule 11: size an
    experiment against a measured noise floor before buying it, and do not use
    section 30's ranges as standard deviations.
