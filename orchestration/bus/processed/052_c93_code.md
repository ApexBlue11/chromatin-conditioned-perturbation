## A. The amendments (RESULTS 93.6), outcome-free, for compute
1. **H3's `min_child_samples`** is chosen by grouped 4-fold over the 11 covered fitting cells, not per-cell LOCO. The folds come
   from a seeded permutation and are dealt round-robin. Each fold's μ excludes the fold, and its features (N1 included) come from
   the fold's training cells only.
2. **H3's fitting sample** is a seeded 5 % of (row, gene) pairs, not 20 %. It is the same in every arm, fold and calibration case.
- **Why:** per-cell LOCO inside every calibration draw at 20 % would exceed a 12 h session.
- **Estimate:** 1,209 fits at ≈ 3 s, ≈ 1 h.

## B. The code (commit above)
- **`model/v9/chromatin_h2.py`** (PI glue): for k ∈ {4, 6, 8}, 5 seeded subsets each, plus all 11 cells. `run_t1` is refitted
  on each subset (its LOCO and N1 over the same subset). It records FBC − N1 and FBC − FB on the drug-known dev rows, with M2's
  planted P1 at 0.5 % (draw 0) on the same subsets.
- **`model/v9/chromatin_gbm.py`** (W30, with PI fixes, the record in AGENT_REGISTRY):
  - `run_h3(ctx, y, make_B)` takes a builder factory `make_B(fit_cells)`, so fold features never use fold cells;
  - `h3_reading` is T1's conjuncts plus **S(C) − S(N1) ≥ t1/2** and C > N1 in ≥ 4 cells;
  - `calibrate` runs draw by draw. Its cases are null; P1 and P2 at {0.5, 2, 5} %; P3 at 5 %; and G (gene-generic: the same
    vector in every covered cell, dev included) at {2, 5} %;
  - `faults_and_mde`: VOID_null, VOID_P3, VOID_G; MDE = the smallest π passing in 3 of 3 draws;
  - the marker carries the file sha1 and `ctx.inputs`.
- **`model/v9/read_chromatin93.py`:** the only reader. It refuses on a marker sha1 mismatch.
  - **H2:** RISING iff the medians of FBC − N1 increase at every step and the rise from k = 4 to all exceeds 2 sd (k = 4).
  - **H3:** VOID, then ADVANCES, then DOES NOT ADVANCE with its label. The null is informative iff MDE_P1 ≤ 2 %.
- **`orchestration/make_chromatin93_kernels.py`:** two CPU kernels with 10 pinned input sha1s (chromatin_funnel `c56bb6f1`,
  unchanged since §91) plus the split bundle. Each writes a completion marker, and records the lightgbm version used.
- **Tests:** 11 pass (`test_chromatin_gbm.py` 7, `test_chromatin_h2.py` 2, `test_read_chromatin93.py` 2). They run on
  synthetic worlds only.
