    Chromatin final: +0.0004 on the right estimand, a clean null rather than a negative
    
    RESULTS 55. Review 002 reproduced every number in packet 002 independently from
    the raw npz and then caught section 54 committing the mirror image of the error
    section 51 had just retracted. Three load-bearing challenges, all verified
    numerically before acceptance.
    
    The 8-cell unweighted mean of -0.0141 draws 102 percent of its value from the
    three cell lines that have no chromatin track at all. Those are cells where the
    treatment does not exist, so they cannot carry information about whether
    chromatin helps, and weighting them as three of eight clusters is what
    manufactured the negative. Restricted to the five cells where chromatin is
    actually present -- the estimand the objective asks for -- the unweighted per-cell
    mean is +0.000360 with a cluster interval of -0.005433 to +0.006153, two of five
    positive, sign test p = 1.000. A tight, well-centred null, and the number that
    belongs in the paper.
    
    Section 54.2's claim that both arms see identical zeros on the placebo rows is
    false, in the docstring and in the results text, and it is my error. E_final for
    HS578T is all-zero and BJAB and H1975 are absent from the cell index, so the ON
    arm feeds zeros there -- but xpert_arm.py sets E and r to the training mean for
    every row, so the ABLATED arm feeds a non-zero in-distribution constant on those
    same rows. The inputs differ and the direction handicaps the ON arm, because
    zeros are out of distribution for a model trained mostly on real chromatin. So
    -0.0226 is a noise floor plus a handicap and it overstates the floor by an
    unknown amount. Withdrawn.
    
    Five defensible estimators of the same effect span two signs and a fourfold
    magnitude range: +0.0042 size-weighted, -0.0141 unweighted, -0.0060 trimmed,
    -0.0038 drop-one, +0.0004 treated-only. With eight clusters no directional
    statement survives the choice, which is method rule 13: fix the estimand and its
    estimator before computing the number, and keep units with no treatment out of a
    treatment estimate.
    
    And the per-cell pattern tracks cell size rather than chromatin coverage.
    Spearman of row count against effect is 0.762 at p = 0.028, the two positive
    cells are the two largest, and there is no dose-response in track count -- three
    tracks gives -0.0018 against two tracks at +0.0036. If chromatin were doing the
    work, three-track cells should lead. A reason to stop rather than to run another
    experiment.
    
    Chromatin's arc closes: +0.0029, then +0.0038 at matched lambda, then +0.0042
    retracted, then -0.0141 on the wrong estimand, and finally +0.0004 as a clean
    null on the correct one. The novelty claim is untouched; there is no benefit to
    claim. Stating it as 'chromatin hurts' would repeat section 54's error a third
    time.
    
    Reviewer calibration continues to be worth recording. It cut against its own C2
    by pointing out that HT29, which is partially covered and should therefore carry
    the handicap, is the most positive cell. And for the second review running it
    hunted a bug, failed to find one, and reported the passing check rather than
    manufacturing a finding. Tally across two reviews: 23 of 24 challenges upheld,
    the single miss caused by a packet defect.
