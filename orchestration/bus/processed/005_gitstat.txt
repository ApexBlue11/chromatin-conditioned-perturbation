    RESULTS 60.8-60.10: the null-key control, which looked like a failure for ten minutes
    
    Ran the identical 2x2 with --key x_cell -- the cell control expression, which drug self-attention has
    no path to. Rows, chunks and weights verified identical between the two runs, not assumed: the row
    index arrays and the per-row r11 vectors are byte-identical on all three splits. Zero GPU-hours.
    
    On the pre-committed estimand the control is NOT null: +0.00616 at 4.1 sigma on the primary split and
    +0.00519 at 6.2 sigma on unseen_both. Read that way, the operator has a non-specific component of
    ~0.006 and the atom interaction at 1.5x that is not cleanly above it. That was the conclusion for
    about ten minutes.
    
    The per-row vectors say the contamination is entirely in the mean's tail. The control's +0.00616
    sits on a distribution whose median is +0.00016 and whose rows split 50.5/49.5 with skew +1.58 --
    no location shift at all. The atoms' -0.00938 sits on a distribution whose median is -0.00803 and
    whose rows split 42/58. Under a sign test, which ignores magnitude, the control is a textbook null
    on the primary split at p=0.70 and the atom effect is p=1.7e-09. The control passes, read as
    location. And its contamination is POSITIVE where the atom interaction is NEGATIVE, so it would mask
    a negative effect rather than manufacture one.
    
    60.9 states the round at the strength the evidence supports: the atom main effect under full
    attention and the specificity of the negative interaction both hold; the stratum-dependent SIGN of
    the interaction (+8.4 sigma on unseen_cell against -4.7 sigma on unseen_compound) is an open
    question, not a result.
    
    Method rule 16: a paired mean over per-row correlation differences is tail-sensitive, so never report
    it alone -- median and sign test on the same rows beside it, and the null-key control through all
    three. That pair of statistics reversed a conclusion twice in one afternoon, so the per-row dump is
    now unconditional.

    RESULTS 60.6/60.7: the estimator question answers itself from the npz, and my 60.3 diagnosis was wrong
    
    I attributed the 8x gap between the two interaction estimators to skew in the per-row double
    differences. The per-row dump says otherwise. Mean and median of the paired contrast AGREE on every
    split (unseen_compound -0.00938 vs -0.00803), so the gap is not about shape at all -- it is the
    difference between the median of the paired contrast and a difference of four independently-taken
    medians, which discards the pairing and is simply a noisier estimator of the same quantity. A
    Wilcoxon signed-rank test, which assumes nothing about shape, gives p=1.4e-07 on unseen_compound
    and p=1.3e-30 on unseen_cell, with unseen_both a clean null at p=0.072.
    
    So 58.2 picked the right estimand even though 58.1 justified it only on variance, and the
    wide-spanning -0.00116 is not a competing estimate. 60.3 is kept as written with the correction
    noted beside it.
    
    60.7 is the part that survives every objection in 60.2-60.4 because it never touches S10 or S00:
    under full attention the atom tokens hurt on two rows in three on both compound splits
    (-0.01901 mean / -0.01129 median on unseen_compound, 34.0% of rows helped; -0.01434 / -0.01414 on
    unseen_both, 33.7%). Mean, median and difference-of-medians agree in sign and magnitude. Giving the
    drug tokens in-loop self-attention did not make the per-atom features worth their place.
